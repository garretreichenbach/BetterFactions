# Federations

A **federation** is a bloc of factions with a chair, a charter and votes. Every member is allied with every other member. A faction can belong to one federation at a time, and can always leave.

Every federation action needs the **Speak for us in the federation** rank right.

## Founding and Joining

On the **Federation** tab, a faction in no federation sees every bloc on the server.

- **FOUND ONE** starts a new federation with you as chair.
- Invitations appear on the list with **ACCEPT** and **NO**.

## The Chair

The chair invites and expels members, declares war, changes the charter, renames and dissolves the federation, unless the charter puts a decision to a vote. The chair can hand over to another member.

If the chair leaves, the longest-standing member takes over. The charter can also rotate the chair every 7, 30 or 90 days.

## The Charter

Set on the **Governance** pane:

| Rule | Options |
|------|---------|
| **Put to a vote** | Admitting, expelling, war, charter changes. Each one separately |
| **Threshold** | Simple majority, two thirds, or everyone |
| **Weighting** | One faction one vote, or weighted by power |
| **War binds** | Every member, or only those who voted for it |
| **Chair term** | None, 7, 30 or 90 days |

Put charter changes to a vote, or the chair can switch every other vote off alone.

## Votes

A vote stays open for 24 hours and closes early once the outcome is certain. Several can be open at once, one per question. The faction that calls a vote votes yes automatically. A faction being voted out cannot vote on it.

## War

A federation war signs a war from each bound member to the target. After that it is each member's own war, and each member makes its own peace.

## Leaving

**LEAVE** ends your alliance with every member. A federation with no members left dissolves.
