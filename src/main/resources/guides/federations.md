# Federations

A **federation** is a bloc of factions with a chair, a charter and votes. Every member is allied with every other member. A faction can belong to one federation at a time, and can always leave.

Every federation action needs the **Speak for us in the federation** rank right.

## Founding and Joining

On the **Federation** tab, a faction in no federation sees every bloc on the server.

- **FOUND** starts a new federation with you as chair.
- Invitations appear on the list with **ACCEPT** and **NO**.

## The Chair

The chair invites and expels members, declares war, makes peace, changes the charter, renames and dissolves the federation, unless the charter puts a decision to a vote.

Each member's row on the member list has its own buttons: **CANCEL** withdraws an invitation, **EXPEL** (or **VOTE OUT**) removes a member, and **CHAIR** hands the chair to them. Expelling, handing over the chair, leaving and dissolving all ask you to confirm first.

INVITE, DECLARE WAR and MAKE PEACE open a list of factions to choose from.

If the chair leaves, the longest-standing member takes over. The charter can also rotate the chair every 7, 30 or 90 days.

## The Charter

Set on the **Governance** pane:

| Rule | Options |
|------|---------|
| **Put to a vote** | Admitting, expelling, war and peace, charter changes. Each one separately |
| **Threshold** | Simple majority, two thirds, or everyone |
| **Weighting** | One faction one vote, or weighted by power |
| **War binds** | Every member, or only those who voted for it |
| **Chair term** | None, 7, 30 or 90 days |

Put charter changes to a vote, or the chair can switch every other vote off alone.

## Votes

A vote stays open for 24 hours and closes early once the outcome is certain. Several can be open at once, one per question. The faction that calls a vote votes yes automatically. A faction being voted out cannot vote on it.

## War

A federation war signs a war from each bound member to the target. After that it is each member's own war.

**EMBARGO** closes trade with a faction from every bound member, as if each had done it alone, and **LIFT EMBARGO** (shown while any member has one) reopens it. Both follow the same voting and binding rules as war. A member lifts only an embargo it imposed itself, and can still lift its own at any time.

**MAKE PEACE** appears while any member is at war. It sends a white peace offer to the enemy from every bound member at war with it. The enemy answers each offer in its diplomacy inbox, so it can make peace with some members and keep fighting others. A member can still make its own peace at any time.

## Leaving

**LEAVE** ends your alliance with every member. A federation with no members left dissolves.
