# Factions

Factions let players group together, protect their ships and stations, sign treaties and organise with ranks and permissions.

> This server runs **BetterFactions**, which replaces faction diplomacy with treaties, opinion and federations. See the **BetterFactions** section of the guides for the full picture.

## The Faction Panel

Press `H` to open the Faction Panel.

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Overview** | Your faction at a glance: members, systems, power, points, news feed and pending offers |
| **Assets** | Your faction's stations, shops, fleets and claimed systems |
| **Members** | Member list, invites and ranks |
| **Diplomacy** | Every other faction, your treaties with them and what they think of you |
| **Federation** | Blocs of factions that share a chair, a charter and votes |
| **Settings** | Create or leave a faction, identity, ranks and standing orders |

## Creating or Joining a Faction

To create a faction: open **Settings** → **CREATE** → enter a name.

To join: accept an invitation from the **Overview** tab (the **INVITES** button appears when you have one), or join a faction that is open to join.

## Faction Options

In **Settings** → **Standing orders**:

| Option | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Open to join | Anyone can join without an invitation |
| Auto-declare war | Declares war on whoever attacks your faction |
| Attack neutrals | Treats all factionless entities as enemies |

## Ranks and Permissions

Every member has a **rank**. Open the rank editor from **Members** → **RANKS** or **Settings** → **EDIT RANKS**: it shows every rank as a column and every permission as a row. Click a rank's name to rename it.

A faction starts with five ranks and can have from 2 to 10. The founder adds a rank with **+ RANK**, which creates a new lowest rank. **- RANK** removes the lowest rank and moves its members up one. New members join at the lowest rank. Ranks are only added or removed at the bottom, so ships, stations and fleets keep the same access when the ladder changes.

| Permission | Grants |
|-----------|--------|
| **Invite players** | Invite new players |
| **Kick members** | Kick members of lower rank |
| **Edit ranks** | Change rank permissions. Nobody can grant a right their own rank lacks |
| **Claim systems** | Claim systems for the faction |
| **Set the homebase** | Choose or reset the homebase |
| **Post news and description** | Edit the faction description and post news |
| **Declare war and peace** | Declare war, make peace and answer every kind of offer |
| **Sign treaties** | Sign or break any treaty except war and peace |
| **Speak for us in the federation** | Act for the faction in its federation |
| **View holdings** | See the Assets tab |

The founder rank holds all of them.

## Diplomacy

Wars, pacts, alliances and peace are all **treaties** now, proposed and answered from the **Diplomacy** tab. See **BetterFactions → Treaties** and **BetterFactions → Opinion and NPC Factions**.

### Personal Enemies

You can declare war against a **single player** rather than their whole faction: **Settings** → **Personal enemies** → **MANAGE**.

## Faction Modules: Claiming Ships and Stations

To assign a ship or station to your faction:

1. Place a **Faction Module** on the structure.
2. Press `R` on it.
3. Enter your **faction signature** (visible in the faction panel).

While the faction module exists and is not destroyed, only you and your faction members can use that structure.

## Homebase

A station claimed by a faction can be designated as the faction's **Homebase** (only one per faction).

> **The homebase and everything docked to it are invulnerable to damage.**

This makes the homebase the safest location for your most valuable assets. Choose its location and defences carefully. To give it up: **Settings** → **RESET** homebase.

## Faction Chat

Send messages only to faction members by typing `/f ` before your message in chat.
